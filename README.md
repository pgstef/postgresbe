# PostgreSQL Users Group Belgium 🐘

![PostgreSQL Users Group Belgium](assets/logo.png)

Welcome to the **PostgreSQL Users Group Belgium** — a community for PostgreSQL enthusiasts, professionals, and newcomers across Belgium. We bring together developers, DBAs, and data engineers to share knowledge, best practices, and a love for open-source databases.

## 🗓️ Stay Connected

Follow us for event announcements, news, and community updates:

- [Meetup.com](https://www.meetup.com/postgresbe)
- [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/111058275)

## 🎤 Events

We organize and participate in major PostgreSQL events in Belgium and beyond:

| Event | Description |
|---|---|
| [PGConf Belgium](https://pgconf.be/) | Our flagship annual PostgreSQL conference in Belgium |
| [FOSDEM PGDay](https://fosdempgday.org/) | A co-located PostgreSQL day at the renowned FOSDEM conference in Brussels |

## 🤝 Get Involved

Whether you're a seasoned PostgreSQL expert or just getting started, everyone is welcome! Here's how you can participate:

- **Attend** one of our meetups or conferences
- **Speak** at an event — we're always looking for new voices and topics
- **Sponsor** — help us grow the community
